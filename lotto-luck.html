🖐 Mage Hand Action Cantrip tap for details
A spectral, floating hand appears at a point you choose within 30 feet. The hand lasts for 1 minute or until you dismiss it.

You can use it to manipulate an object, open an unlocked door or container, stow or retrieve an item from an open container, or pour the contents of a vial. You can move the hand up to 30 feet each time you use it.

Can't attack, activate magic items, or carry more than 10 lbs.
🃏 Prestidigitation Action Cantrip tap for details
A minor magical trick. Choose one effect each time you cast:

• Create an instantaneous, harmless sensory effect (shower of sparks, puff of wind, faint music, odd odor)
• Instantaneously light or snuff a candle, torch, or small campfire
• Instantaneously clean or soil an object no larger than 1 cubic foot
• Chill, warm, or flavor up to 1 cubic foot of nonliving material for 1 hour
• Make a color, small mark, or symbol appear on an object or surface for 1 hour
• Create a nonmagical trinket or illusory image that fits in your hand and lasts until end of next turn

Up to 3 effects active at once. No two instances of this spell can create the same effect simultaneously.
💬 Message Action Cantrip tap for details
Point your finger toward a creature within 120 feet and whisper a message. The target (and only the target) hears the message and can reply in a whisper that only you can hear.

You can cast this spell through solid objects if you are familiar with the target and know it is beyond the barrier. Magical silence blocks the spell. It doesn't have to follow a straight line and can travel freely around corners or through openings.

Range: 120ft · Duration: 1 round · No save, no attack roll.
😈 Thaumaturgy Action Tiefling tap for details
Manifest a minor wonder. Choose one effect — lasts up to 1 minute (up to 3 simultaneous effects):

• Your voice booms up to 3× as loud as normal
• Flames flicker, brighten, dim, or change color
• Harmless tremors in the ground
• Create an instantaneous sound originating from a point within 30ft (thunder rumble, raven's caw, eerie whispers)
• Your eyes glow or change appearance
• Cause an unlocked door or window to fly open or slam shut

Purely cosmetic. Great for intimidation and theatrics at the table.

😈 Tiefling Racial Traits▼
Darkvision: You can see in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern colour in darkness, only shades of grey.

Hellish Resistance: You have resistance to fire damage (take half damage from all fire sources).

Infernal Legacy:
  • Thaumaturgy (cantrip, always available)
  • Hellish Rebuke — 1/day as a reaction when damaged: attacker takes 2d10 fire damage (DEX save DC 11 for half). Uses CHA for spell save.
  • Darkness — 1/day at 5th level (not yet).

Languages: Common, Infernal.
🃏 Charlatan Background▼
Skill Proficiencies: Deception, Sleight of Hand
Tool Proficiencies: Disguise Kit, Forgery Kit

False Identity: You have a second identity including documentation, an established history, and a disguise. You can also forge documents — letters, decrees, contracts. You can't forge magic items or a personal effect belonging to another person (like their signature without a sample).

Favourite Schemes (flavour): Rigged games of chance, selling worthless goods, short cons at the gaming tables.
✦ Sorcerer Class Features (Level 4)▼
Sorcerous Origin — Wild Magic:
  • Wild Magic Surge: After you cast a levelled sorcerer spell, the DM can have you roll a d20. On a 1, roll on the Wild Magic Surge table.
  • Tides of Chaos (1/LR): Gain advantage on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw. Afterwards, the DM can have you roll on the surge table at any time before you regain this feature — doing so recharges it immediately.

Font of Magic: You have 4 Sorcery Points (resets on long rest).
  • Converting Slots → SP: As a bonus action, expend a spell slot to gain SP equal to the slot's level.
  • Converting SP → Slots: As a bonus action, spend SP to create a slot: 1st = 2 SP · 2nd = 3 SP · 3rd = 5 SP.

Metamagic (choose 2 at level 3):
  • Quickened Spell (2 SP): Change a 1-action cast time to a bonus action. If you cast a levelled spell as a bonus action, you can only cast a cantrip with your action that turn.
  • Subtle Spell (1 SP): Cast without verbal or somatic components. Cannot be detected or countered by Counterspell.

Ability Score Improvement (Level 4): +2 to one ability score or +1 to two.
🎲 Spell Reference▼
Spell Attack Bonus: +6  ·  Spell Save DC: 14

🔥 Fire Bolt (Cantrip) — Ranged spell attack +6, range 120ft. 1d10 fire damage on hit. Ignites flammable unattended objects.

🎲 Chaos Bolt (1st+) — Ranged spell attack +6, range 120ft. 2d8+1d6 damage; first d8 determines type (1=Acid 2=Cold 3=Fire 4=Force 5=Lightning 6=Poison 7=Psychic 8=Thunder). If both d8s match, bolt leaps to new target within 30ft. Upcast: +1d6 per slot above 1st.

🛡 Shield (1st, Reaction) — Triggered when hit by attack or targeted by Magic Missile. +5 AC until start of your next turn, including against the triggering attack. Blocks Magic Missile automatically.

💤 Sleep (1st+) — Range 90ft, 20ft radius. No save. Roll 5d8 HP pool; creatures fall unconscious starting with the lowest current HP until the pool is exhausted. Lasts 1 min; woken by damage or shaking. Undead and charm-immune creatures unaffected. Upcast: +2d8 per slot above 1st.

🪞 Mirror Image (2nd, No Concentration) — Creates 3 illusory duplicates for 1 minute. When attacked, roll d20 to see if a copy is hit instead: 3 copies = 6+, 2 copies = 8+, 1 copy = 11+. Hit copy is destroyed. No effect against blind attackers, truesight, or non-sight targeting (tremorsense, etc.).

✦ Misty Step (2nd, Bonus Action) — Teleport up to 30ft to an unoccupied space you can see. No attack roll or save. Verbal component only (note: Subtle Spell removes this).

⚔ Equipment & AC▼
Unarmoured AC: 10 + DEX mod = 12
With Mage Armor (not known): 13 + DEX = 15

Arcane Focus: Deck of Cards — used in place of material components for all spells.

Disguise Kit: Contains cosmetics, hair dye, small props, and a few character costumes. A check using this kit is opposed by the observer's WIS (Insight) vs your CHA (Deception).

Forgery Kit: Contains inks, parchments, seals, and scribing tools. Forging requires a sample of the original handwriting or seal. Opposed by INT (Investigation) to detect.
⚠ Condition Reference▼
Blinded: Can't see. Auto-fail sight checks. Attack rolls against you have adv; your attacks have disadv.
Charmed: Can't attack charmer. Charmer has adv on social checks vs you.
Deafened: Can't hear. Auto-fail hearing checks.
Frightened: Disadv on ability checks and attacks while source of fear is in sight. Can't willingly move closer.
Grappled: Speed becomes 0.
Incapacitated: Can't take actions or reactions.
Invisible: Can't be seen. Attacks against you have disadv; your attacks have adv.
Paralyzed: Incapacitated, can't move or speak. Auto-fail STR/DEX saves. Attacks have adv; hits within 5ft are crits.
Poisoned: Disadv on attack rolls and ability checks.
Prone: Disadv on attacks. Attacks within 5ft have adv; ranged attacks have disadv. Must use half speed to stand.
Restrained: Speed 0. Disadv on attacks and DEX saves. Attacks against you have adv.
Stunned: Incapacitated, can't move, can only speak haltingly. Auto-fail STR/DEX saves. Attacks have adv.
Unconscious: Incapacitated, can't move or speak, unaware. Drop items, fall prone. Auto-fail STR/DEX saves. Attacks have adv; hits within 5ft are crits.
